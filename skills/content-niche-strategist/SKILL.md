# Content Niche Strategist

## Purpose
"Pick a niche and post consistently" is not a strategy. This skill produces a concrete positioning decision - a specific angle within a niche, evidence there's room for it, and a monetization plan sequenced to the creator's current audience size, because different monetization paths become viable at different stages.

## When to use
The user is deciding what niche/angle to build a channel or account around, or already has an audience and wants a monetization plan.

## Process
1. **Establish inputs**: the creator's existing skills/knowledge/interests (a sustainable niche needs a genuine unfair advantage or genuine interest, not just "this niche seems profitable"), current audience size and platform if any, and time/budget available.
2. **Map the competitive landscape** using research tools available: identify 4-6 established creators/accounts in the space, note their angle, apparent audience size, and content format - to find where the space is saturated versus where a specific sub-angle is underserved.
3. **Recommend a positioning angle**: a specific intersection (e.g. not "fitness" but "strength training for people over 40 returning after injury") that's narrow enough to be defensible but broad enough to sustain content volume. State explicitly why this angle has room, citing the competitive gaps found.
4. **Sequence a monetization mix to audience stage**, not just list every option:
   - Early stage (small/no audience): affiliate links, own digital products/services if credibility exists offline - sponsorship deals are usually not realistic yet.
   - Growing stage: platform ad revenue becomes meaningful, first sponsorships (smaller/product-based deals), continued affiliate/products.
   - Established stage: premium sponsorships, own product lines, memberships/communities.
   Only recommend what fits the audience size actually stated - do not suggest sponsorship strategy to someone with 200 followers.
5. **Flag one validation step** before heavy investment (e.g. "post 10 pieces in this angle and check saves/shares before committing 6 months") so the recommendation is testable, not a leap of faith.

## Output format
- Recommended niche/angle + rationale
- Competitive landscape summary (4-6 comparables with gap analysis)
- Monetization roadmap by audience stage
- One validation step before full commitment

## Guardrails
Do not promise specific income figures or timelines to profitability - creator monetization outcomes vary enormously and depend on factors (luck, timing, execution quality) this skill cannot assess. If web research tools are unavailable, say so explicitly and base the competitive landscape on the user's own knowledge of the space rather than fabricating named competitors or numbers.
