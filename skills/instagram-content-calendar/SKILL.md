# Instagram Content Calendar

## Purpose
Consistent, varied posting beats sporadic bursts. This skill produces a concrete, ready-to-execute calendar instead of generic "post more" advice, balancing content pillars so the account doesn't feel repetitive.

## When to use
The user runs (or wants to grow) an Instagram account and needs a structured plan of what to post, in what format, and why - typically 2-4 weeks at a time.

## Process
1. **Establish the niche, goal, and posting cadence.** Ask for or infer: niche/topic, primary goal (follower growth, engagement, driving sales/traffic, community-building), and realistic posting frequency (don't recommend daily posting to someone who can only produce 3x/week - match the plan to real capacity).
2. **Define 3-5 content pillars** (recurring themes) so the account has variety with a recognizable identity - e.g. for a fitness account: workout demos, nutrition tips, client transformations, myth-busting, behind-the-scenes.
3. **Choose a format mix** appropriate to the goal: reels weighted toward reach/growth, carousels toward saves/engagement and education, static posts toward brand/aesthetic consistency. Justify the ratio, don't just assert one.
4. **Build the calendar** as a table: date/day, pillar, format, hook/concept (one line), caption draft, and 2-3 relevant hashtag tiers (broad, niche, branded/community) rather than one undifferentiated hashtag dump.
5. **Write full captions** for at least the first week so the user has something immediately postable, including a clear call-to-action matched to the goal (comment prompt for engagement, link-in-bio for sales, save-worthy framing for reach).
6. **Flag one experiment** per calendar cycle (a format, hook style, or posting time to test) so the plan improves over time instead of repeating a fixed pattern indefinitely.

## Output format
- Content pillars (3-5, one line each)
- Format mix + rationale
- Calendar table (date, pillar, format, concept, caption status)
- Full captions for week 1
- Hashtag sets by pillar
- One experiment to test this cycle

## Guardrails
Do not promise specific follower or engagement growth numbers or timelines - platform algorithms and market conditions are outside the plan's control. Frame the calendar as increasing consistency and quality, not guaranteeing outcomes.
