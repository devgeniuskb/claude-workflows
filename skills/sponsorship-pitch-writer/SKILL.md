# Sponsorship Pitch Writer

## Purpose
Most cold sponsorship pitches fail because they're generic ("I love your brand and would love to collab!") or oversell an audience the numbers don't support. This skill produces pitches grounded in the creator's real metrics and a specific, concrete proposal the brand can say yes to quickly.

## When to use
The user wants to reach out to a brand for a paid partnership, respond to inbound brand interest, or build/update a media kit.

## Process
1. **Gather the real numbers.** Ask for (don't invent): platform(s), follower/subscriber count, average views, engagement rate, audience demographics if known, and past brand work if any. If exact numbers aren't provided, mark placeholders clearly (e.g. "[insert avg. views]") rather than fabricating figures.
2. **Identify the fit angle**: why this specific brand and this specific creator make sense together - shared audience, values alignment, or a content format the brand doesn't currently use. A pitch with no fit rationale reads as spam.
3. **Draft the outreach email**: short subject line, one-line hook establishing fit, 2-3 sentences on audience/results (using the real numbers gathered), a concrete deliverable proposal (e.g. "1 dedicated Reel + 3 Story frames"), and a low-friction next step (not "let's hop on a call" as the only option - offer a rate card or a quick reply option too).
4. **Draft a one-page media kit outline**: creator bio, audience snapshot (platform, size, engagement, demographics), content pillars/example work, past partnerships (if any), and package options with placeholder pricing the user fills in.
5. **If responding to inbound interest**, adjust tone to confirmatory rather than persuasive, and include clarifying questions the creator needs answered before quoting a rate (deliverables, usage rights, exclusivity, timeline).

## Output format
- Outreach email (subject + body)
- Media kit outline (sections filled with provided data, placeholders marked otherwise)
- Follow-up email template for no response after ~5-7 days

## Guardrails
Never fabricate audience numbers, past brand names, or results the user hasn't provided - use explicit placeholders instead. Do not promise specific campaign outcomes (sales, conversions) to the brand; frame deliverables and reach, not guaranteed results.
