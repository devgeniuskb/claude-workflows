# Market Research & Persona Builder

## Purpose
Personas built from stereotypes ("Sarah, 32, busy mom") are decoration, not decision-making tools. This skill grounds the persona in actual research where tools allow it, and where research isn't available, builds working hypotheses explicitly labeled as such - so the user knows what to trust and what to validate.

## When to use
The user needs to define or understand their target customer for a product, campaign, or business decision.

## Process
1. **Clarify the product/offer and the research question**: what decision will this persona inform (messaging, feature prioritization, ad targeting, channel choice)? The persona's depth and focus should match what it's for.
2. **Research the market** using available tools (web search) when possible: existing competitor customer reviews, forum/community discussions where the target audience talks about their problems, industry reports. Cite what was actually found versus reasoned from general knowledge.
3. **Build the persona around jobs-to-be-done**, not just demographics: what situation triggers the need, what they're actually trying to accomplish, and what "success" looks like to them - demographics matter less than the underlying motivation and are often reasoned from research, not observed directly.
4. **Document buying triggers and objections** specifically: the event/realization that makes them start looking for a solution, and the concrete hesitations that stall a purchase decision (price, trust, switching cost, "I'll just keep doing it manually") - objections are usually more useful for messaging than any demographic detail.
5. **Note where the persona is evidence-backed versus a working assumption.** If real research (reviews, forum threads, survey data) supports a claim, say so and reference it; if it's a reasonable inference with no direct evidence, label it clearly so the user knows to validate it (e.g. via customer interviews) before betting a campaign on it.
6. **Suggest the cheapest validation step** - typically talking to 5-10 real prospective customers - before the persona is used for a high-cost decision.

## Output format
- Research question / decision this persona informs
- Persona: situation/trigger, job-to-be-done, buying triggers, objections
- Evidence log: what's research-backed (with source) vs. working assumption
- Suggested validation step

## Guardrails
Do not present demographic guesses or invented statistics as researched findings - label unverified claims explicitly. If web research tools are unavailable or return nothing useful, say so rather than fabricating market data or fake-sourcing claims.
